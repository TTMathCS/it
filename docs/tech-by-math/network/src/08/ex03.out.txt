   window     64 KiB, RTT   1 ms: at most     524.3 Mbit/s
   window     64 KiB, RTT  20 ms: at most      26.2 Mbit/s
   window     64 KiB, RTT 100 ms: at most       5.2 Mbit/s
   window   1024 KiB, RTT 100 ms: at most      83.9 Mbit/s
   window  16384 KiB, RTT 100 ms: at most   1,342.2 Mbit/s
   to fill 1 Gbit/s at RTT 80 ms the window must be >= bandwidth x delay = 10,000,000 bytes = 9.5 MiB
